Churches in Waco 韦科教会

Baptist and non-denominational congregations drawn from Baylor Spiritual Life's local church list.

Selection: congregations Baylor Spiritual Life lists for the Waco area, limited to Baptist and non-denominational Christian churches that hold to historic Christian teaching. Churches that have left or been removed from the Baptist General Convention of Texas over doctrinal standards are not included. Service times change, so please confirm with each church before visiting.
